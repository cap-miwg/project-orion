How to Read This

What the totals include — and what they don't.

Included

Every retail-priced hardware line from the kit BOMs, plus add-ons and required subscriptions (Starlink, UniFi Talk) at their listed monthly rates.

Excluded

Tax, shipping, optional professional install, items already provided by CAP (staff laptops, comms-trailer chassis, VHF radios, corporate Google Drive, etc.).

Reproducible

Approve at the calculator's number and a Wing can procure at or below that figure from the listed vendors. Pricing is current to 2026 retail.

Air kit

ORION-Air is shown as a concept and excluded from totals. Pricing depends on airframe-specific mounting and operational use cases still under discussion.

Auto-added support gear

When phone count exceeds 2 per Core, or display count exceeds 2 per Base, the calculator automatically adds a UniFi Standard 16 PoE Switch ($299, 8 PoE ports) to handle the extra load.

Beyond the cap

The calculator caps at 10 phones per Core and 10 displays per Base. Larger deployments are possible but require a custom PoE-budget review — reach out and we'll size it.
