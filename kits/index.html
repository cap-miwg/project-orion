THE FIVE KITS

Each kit is a complete, standalone capability. Together they mesh into one logical fabric.

KIT 001

ORION-Core

The Wing HQ anchor. Always-on controller, voice system, and TAK home base — the node every other kit federates back to.
$1,595one-time
KIT 002

ORION-Trailer

Rapid-deploy mobile core. Wi-Fi 6 across the operations area, VoIP, cameras, and shared displays.
$4,995one-time
KIT 003

ORION-Base

Mission base extension. Eight phones on the Wing dial plan, full-area Wi-Fi 6, cameras and signage. In a transit case.
$4,995one-time
KIT 004

ORION-Fleet

Vehicle node. Mobile satellite WAN, in-vehicle Wi-Fi, rugged ATAK tablet. Joins the ORION mesh on arrival.
$2,495one-time
KIT 005

ORION-Air

Aircraft node. In-flight connectivity for the aircrew — imagery uplink, live data, and on-board systems that need internet access.
